JPG Format Example

About This image

This photograph shows a colorful oil canvas in the shape of a diamond. The Italian artist Gino Severini uses different shades and line patterns to achieve this look.

Why JPG Format?

JPG is one of the most common file types for photographs. This image contains a wide variety of colors and JPG can display up to 16.7 million colors. It also uses lossy compression to reduce the file size of an image this large.
