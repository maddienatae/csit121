PNG Format Example

About This image

This photograph shows a colorful paint palette with different kinds of brushes a painter may use. The paint is messy and some colors are mixing together, creating new shades.

Why PNG Format?

PNG works well with this image. It supports millions of colors within the photograph and it uses lossless compression to reduce the file size without losing the quality of the picture. PNG supports the transparency of the checkerboarded background of the image.
